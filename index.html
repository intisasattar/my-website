02 The cost of missed opportunities

The most expensive lead is the one you've already paid for.

Marketing makes the phone ring. What happens next decides the return.

  • A missed call.
  • A follow-up that never comes.
  • A prospect who meant to decide, and didn't.

Each one is small. Together, they are the difference between a good year and an exceptional one.

Fifty enquiries a month, converted at 10%, is five clients. At 20%, it's ten. Same marketing. Twice the business.

03 Our approach

One discipline. Practised exceptionally well.

We don't generate leads, run advertising or sell software. We close. Our closers work as an extension of your business and take each qualified enquiry from first conversation to final decision.

  1. I

    The conversation

    We listen before we advise. We understand what each prospect needs, then show precisely how you deliver it.

  2. II

    The follow-through

    Decisions take time. We stay present, measured and persistent until every prospect reaches a clear answer.

  3. III

    The close

    Concerns met with candour. Decisions made with confidence. Never pressure, always professionalism.

From one in nine to one in four.

The challenge
Around 400 enquiries a month from paid search, answered by a front desk between appointments. One in nine became a booked treatment plan.
What we changed
A dedicated closing team on every enquiry within the hour, a structured 21-day follow-up, and objections answered in the clinic’s own voice.
The result
Conversion rose from 11% to 26% in four months: £1.1m in additional treatment revenue in the first year.

07 FAQ

Before we speak.

What does UG Global do?

We are a specialist sales-closing firm. Our closers work as an extension of your business and take each qualified enquiry from first conversation to final decision: the sales conversation, the follow-up, the objections and the close.

Do you generate leads?

No. We don't generate leads, run advertising or sell software. Our work begins where interest already exists, with the enquiries your marketing already brings in.

Why not hire a salesperson?

You get a trained closing team from the first week, with no recruitment, salaries or management. Closing is our only discipline, so you get depth, not breadth.

How will you represent us?

As you would. Before speaking to a single client, each closer learns your offer, your clients and your voice. Every conversation is held to one standard: would you be proud of it? Your reputation travels with every call we make, and we protect it as our own.

How are you paid?

A monthly fee for your dedicated closing team, plus a success fee on revenue we close. Both are agreed before we begin, and you see every opportunity and outcome in a monthly report.

How soon can we begin?

Every partnership begins with a review of your enquiries, then immersion in your offer, your clients and your voice. Most partnerships go live within two to three weeks of the first conversation.
